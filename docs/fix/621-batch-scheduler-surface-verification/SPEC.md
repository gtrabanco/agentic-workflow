### Reading of S3 (the one ambiguity, resolved from the issue's own evidence)

"Emitted only for the partial-coverage case" names **one** of the three causes
the current field conflates, and the issue's `## Expected behaviour` rules the
other two out of the field: a no-branch issue has "nothing to verify", and a
merged branch's own diff against the trunk is **empty by construction**
(measured, PE-003), so flagging it would report every declared surface of every
open issue whose branch already landed — a false positive generated by the
mechanism, not a fact about the work. The cause discriminator alternative the
issue offers ("or carries a typed cause field under the same bump") is not taken:
it would add a second core field for two causes that carry no merge risk. The
field therefore means exactly what its name says — *a surface the live branch
does not cover, so a merge conflict is possible* — and the third cause (a diff
that could not be computed) becomes a declared `degraded[]` entry, which is S3's
own third clause.
